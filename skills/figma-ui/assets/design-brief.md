## Options and decision evidence

For an open direction, compare two or three meaningful options with equivalent task, content, states, and configurations. Review them against the target findings and success criteria. Record strengths, tradeoffs, selected node IDs, requested refinements, and unresolved gaps. Preserve a settled direction without requiring new options. Distinguish a style-only choice from approval that also covers concrete frames, states, or implementation; honor the actual scope without another approval ceremony.

## Approval record

This template grants no approval. Record the actual approver/authorized delegate, date, decision evidence, selected frame IDs, pinned revision, state/configuration scope, and exclusions before changing manifest status to `approved`. Preserve screenshots, metadata, literal text, assets, and hashes. Retain earlier references as `superseded` when a new approved revision replaces them.
