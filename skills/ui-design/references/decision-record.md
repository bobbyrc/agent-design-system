# Consequential decision record

Keep the existing project brief as the source of task scope. Link a compact decision record from it; use an established equivalent rather than a second brief. This is guidance, not a schema or approval artifact.

Record ID/revision, brief revision or hash, mode, profile ID/version, primary job, fixture identity, integration mode, constraints, non-goals, selected pattern, alternatives, concrete visual direction, required state/acceptance IDs, and existing reference manifest when applicable. Preserve the actual delegated decision scope. Reference approval remains in the reference workflow's own record.

Classify the basis of each choice:

| Class | Example | Required honesty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed | The supplied synthetic records have comparable due dates | Cite the brief, inspected artifact or fixture; observation of a fixture is not user research |
| Inference | Aligned columns should help compare dates | Explain the mechanism, credible alternative, risk and observable check |
| Assumption | Staff are repeat operators | Mark unvalidated; record consequence and how it could be checked |

For each consequential choice record the question, selection, alternatives and reasons, rationale class, risk, and acceptance IDs. Avoid a diary of minor spacing changes. “Modern and premium” cannot settle composition; “aligned due dates, text status, selected-record action” can be inspected.

If evidence contradicts a choice, revise it within authorized scope and invalidate affected checks. Do not change a frozen acceptance contract merely to make a failing candidate pass. An original direct-to-code example may explicitly record `reference_manifest: null` with a reason; reproduction may not invent or omit its required reference identity.
